# Utrecht, Monday 14 September 2026 — what actually happened

The plan page (`utrecht-itinerary.html`) still describes the day as proposed;
`utrecht-map.html` is what actually happened.

**The GPX that this file describes is not in git.** It is a one-second trace of
a real ride, finer than anything the map draws, and this repo is public, so
`data/**/*.gpx` is gitignored. `src/utrecht/build.py` needs it back in this
directory to rebuild the map, and says so if it is missing.

### The first leg, before the recording started

The bike was picked up at **52.095258, 5.115366**, which is **14 m from where
the GPX ends** — so the ride is a loop that returns the Cargoroo to the same
spot it came from. Strava did not start recording until **219 m away** (straight
line), at the GPX's first point, 52.093311, 5.114910.

The leg in between, as described: **out along Waterstraat, turn onto
Sint-Jacobsstraat**, and the recording picks up from there. Overpass confirms
both ends of that description — the pickup point is 14 m from Waterstraat, and
the GPX's first point is 9 m from Sint-Jacobsstraat. Note OSM spells it
**Sint-Jacobsstraat**, hyphenated.

Valhalla was returning 502s when I tried to route this leg, so it has not been
drawn yet. It is a short, unambiguous two-street connection; re-run the router
when building the map. Until then, do not draw the track's first point as the
start of the ride — it is only where the recording woke up.

## Built

`utrecht-map.html`, generated by `src/utrecht/build.py`. Fourteen legs: two on
tram 22 from OSM relation geometry, eleven walking legs from Valhalla, and the
ride itself from this GPX.

The GPX needed cleaning before it would draw well. Strava was running before the
bike moved and kept running after it stopped, so the raw track opens with 26
points and closes with 108 points of sub-metre jitter, which rendered as a blot
at each end. Those are trimmed by radius, the line is ended on the marker rather
than a few metres off it, and the rest is simplified with Douglas-Peucker at
1.5 m: 2,714 points down to 349, 13.03 km drawn against 13.24 km measured. The
distance quoted on the page is the measured one.

The opening 310 m of the ride is routed rather than recorded, and draws solid
like the rest; the panel says so in words instead. That leg is aimed at the
south end of the southbound cycleway on Sint-Jacobsstraat, not at the
recording's own first fix: the fix sits about 27 m east of the cycleway, near
enough the Lange Viestraat junction that Valhalla snapped to it and came around
by Jacobskerkhof and the Oudegracht, three streets nobody rode.
