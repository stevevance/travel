Two miles across the South Loop to the Halsted Street station, __TRAIN__ miles on the train, and on from Route 59 by paths and side streets: __BIKE__ miles on the bike as far as the map goes. High stress is a speed limit of 35 mph or more and no bike infrastructure of any kind, and that is __PCT__% of the riding shown, all of it on North Aurora Road. The map starts and stops at public corners, short of both ends of the trip. The route was drawn by hand: the bicycle lines are that drawing snapped to the streets under it, dashed because nobody recorded them, and the train is the track from OpenStreetMap. The pictures are Google Street View. Click any step to fly there.
