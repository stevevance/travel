Chicago, a South Side loop, in 3D

The ride, by bike

The same -mile loop, with every building within a quarter mile of it raised from OpenStreetMap and the rest of the city cut away; the tinted buildings stand by the stops. Drag to move, right-drag or two fingers to turn and tilt, scroll or shift-drag a box to zoom, click a stop to fly there.
