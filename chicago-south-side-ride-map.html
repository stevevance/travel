Chicago, a South Side loop

The ride, by bike

Down the lakefront to Jackson Park, lunch at Medici on 57th, and back through Washington Park and Kenwood to the lakefront, about 19 miles. The ride was not recorded: the line is Valhalla's bicycle route pinned to the streets and park drives that were taken, drawn dashed because it is a reconstruction rather than a recording, with arrows for the way it went. It starts and ends a few blocks short of home. The camera dots are the day's pictures. Click any step to fly there.
